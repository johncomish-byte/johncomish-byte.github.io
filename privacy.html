Privacy policy

HA Controller is used only by its owner, John Comish, to connect his own Google Calendar to his own Home Assistant system.

  • Calendar data is read and written only by the owner's Home Assistant installation, on hardware he controls.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google user data is used only to show and manage the owner's calendar events in Home Assistant, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or by removing the integration in Home Assistant.
